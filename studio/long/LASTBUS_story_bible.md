# THE LAST BUS — story bible (10 parts, English shorts, 9:16)

**Premise:** 12 strangers. One bus. Ten hours to the last ferry. A bite turns you in about ten hours, and one of them is hiding one.
**Format:** each part is a complete mini-story (a laugh, a scare, a twist) *and* one step of the mystery, so a new viewer can start anywhere and a binge-watcher is rewarded.
**Announcement (standing rule):** the first frame shows the series chip (THE LAST BUS · PART n / 10 · TOPIC) and BUS-E, the bus's cheerful PA system, says it again a few seconds in ("Ding dong! This is the Last Bus. Part three of ten: the last drop."). It signs off with the series name, the part and the next stop. A route line at the bottom (stop n of 10, the clock, the place) stays on screen all the time.

## The cast (each has a voice, a colour and a want)
* **Rosa** (driver, 54) — "Nobody gets on. Nobody gets off. Don't talk to the driver." *The one who is bitten.* Wants to get everyone to Pier 8 before she turns.
* **Priya** (ER nurse) — calm, sarcastic; she does the real first-aid logic.
* **Marcus** (security guard, huge, terrified) — "aggressively cautious"; becomes the driver.
* **Teddy** (9) and **Gary** the plastic dinosaur — says the true things out loud ("Rosa's sweating too").
* **June** (78) — outlived two husbands; the handbag of holding; the cherry pie.
* **Vic** — live-streamer, talks to "chat". **Lena** — deadpan; hides a kitten (Mochi). **Benny** — mascot suit, nineteen, third shift. **Mr. Park** — briefcase; type-one diabetes. **Dante** — has seen every zombie movie and plays every game. **Amara** and baby. **Walt** — asleep through the apocalypse.
* **BUS-E** — the PA: cheerful, literal, accidentally reads out sensor data. **Steve** — the polite zombie with a ticket, in every part ("Where was Steve?").

## The fair-play clue ledger (Rosa)
Symptom list (Part 1): fever · sweating · shaking · confusion · red eyes. Rosa shows all five over the night; every other suspect only shows *one* of them for an innocent reason.
Fever/sweat (2, 3, 4, 6) · shaking hands (3, 7, 8) · confusion, "left, LEFT" (7) · red eyes (7, 8, 9) · refuses the sleeve check (5) · never leaves the seat (3, 6) · the 39 °C seat (4) · "Don't talk to the driver" (1).
Red herrings (each cleared with a real tip): **Benny** (a stain: ketchup), **Lena** (red marks: cat scratches; bite = two curved rows of punctures, scratch = parallel lines), **Mr. Park** (sweating, shaking, confused: low blood sugar; the 15-gram rule), **Marcus** (fear sweat), **Walt** (never moves: asleep).

## Real-world tips hidden in the jokes
Echoes in tunnels (whisper) · light is a loudspeaker · siphoning: never use your mouth, can lower than the tank · bite vs scratch · look-alike symptoms of low blood sugar (about 15 g of fast sugar) · noise is a weapon if you aim it (a decoy) · stay calm, pick one job each.

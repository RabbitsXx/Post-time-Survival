# HOOK FORMULA (owner rule): keep viewers watching

Every short (and every title/cover) is built from these devices, plus others where they fit:

1. **IF YOU ...** speak to the viewer: "If you hear this sound, run." Opening line (first 3 s) addresses the viewer or poses a stake for them.
2. **NUMBERS** tell it in figures: "3 minutes of air", "47 stairs", "1 key". At least one concrete number in the opening 5 s and in the title/cover.
3. **TWIST (plot flip)** at least one reversal per part: what the viewer assumed is wrong ("It was never the zombies..."). Put it around 60-75% of the clip.
4. **OPEN LOOP (cliffhanger)** parts 1 and 2 end on an unanswered question; part 3 ends on a payoff plus a teaser for the next series.
5. **Others as fit**: countdown/timer, secret withheld till the end, "most people get this wrong", contrast (small vs huge), a rule the viewer can test, a promise ("watch for the trick at the end").

Gates: pitchlint needs a `**Hook plan**` field naming the YOU / NUMBER / TWIST / LOOP of each part; dialoguelint scores the built clip (dept `writing`).

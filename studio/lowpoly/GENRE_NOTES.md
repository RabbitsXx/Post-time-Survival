# Genre notes: popular low-poly "true story / weird fact" Shorts (vidIQ data, 2026-10-31)
Sources: top 50 Shorts of the reference channel (views, length, titles) + vidIQ outlier search of 25 similar Shorts from other channels. Numbers are as reported by vidIQ.

## 1. What wins on the reference channel (top 50 by views)
- Biggest hits: "You've Never Touched Anything" 53M (physics concept), "Weirdest Nuclear Code Proposal" 50M (dark history), "He Wore Upside-Down Glasses For 124 Days" 36M (experiment), "A Cow With A Window" 29M (animal/vet), "How Anglerfish Do The Thing" 27M, "The Ganzfeld Effect" 27M (mind), "Brain Cells Can Play Pong" 26M (science).
- Funny-crime stories do less (Lemon Juice Bank Robbery 11M, Dumbest Burglars 11M). Science / body / mind / animal oddities and strange-but-true history lead.
- Length: 25-42 s for nearly all top-10 (median ~32 s); 1-minute clips rank lower. Engagement 2-3% likes/views.
- Title patterns in the top 50: noun phrase ("A Cow With A Window", "Kentucky Meat Shower", "The Bubble Boy") ~14; "He/She + odd specific" ~8 ("...For 124 Days", "...A Paperclip For A House"); "Why You Shouldn't/Should/Get..." ~8; mind/science concept ~7; crime/dumb story ~4; "How ..." ~2. Specific numbers help (124 days, 30,000 year old).
- Descriptions carry a factual paragraph + hashtags; tags almost unused (see CHANNEL_META.md).

Kruschiki Supply Co (99K): "A daring mission to capture a Soviet helicopter" 212K, 1:39. "He played CS for 30 hours and unboxed a $200,000 knife" (No Proof, 5.6K subs) 2.4M, 55 s (gaming-adjacent).
- Lesson: small channels hit 0.4-2M with ONE strong story plus a curiosity title ("They Declared War On X And Lost", "This X Did Y For 7 Years"). The lane is getting crowded with copycats (even "How Toy Story 2 Almost Didn't Exist" is re-made).

## 3. Decisions for PosttimeLowPoly
1. Topic mix: ~50% science/body/mind/animal oddities, ~30% strange-but-true history, ~20% funny stories. (Our first 10 ideas were history-heavy.)
2. Do not copy topics already done by the reference or PolySoldier. Emu War is taken (PolySoldier, 3 weeks ago, 589K) -> deprioritised. Also avoid: cow window, upside-down glasses, lemon juice, sunfish, Toy Story 2, meat shower, honey man, paperclip house.
3. Differentiators: Poly (recurring deadpan character), sources named in every description, "theory vs fact" honesty, a final one-word/one-line punchline ("Stackable.", "The pig was not consulted.").
4. Length 22-35 s. Hook = the strangest fact in line 1, visible in shot 1. Titles 3-7 words; prefer a specific number or an odd verb; rotate the 3 winning shapes (noun phrase / "He did X for N days" / "Why You Shouldn't X").
5. Cover: Poly's deadpan face + the topic's object, <=5 words.

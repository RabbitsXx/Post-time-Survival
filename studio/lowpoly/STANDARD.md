# Clip standard (PosttimeLowPoly): how a clip is made, in order. Nothing is rendered or posted before its gate passes.

0. **Topic and facts** — open the Wikipedia raw text (`curl "https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=X&action=raw"`; web search summaries are not enough). Write `topics/<id>.md`: one row per spoken line = status + source. Say "named after", "allegedly", "claimed" when that is what the source says; no claim that only appears in a search summary.
1. **Hook** (HOOKS.md) — frame 0 is mid-action; sentence 1 is the strangest TRUE statement; a hit/pop/jerk by ~1 s; no card, no title, no series name in the first 3 s.
2. **Models first** — every important model is rendered as a 4-angle turntable plus a thumbnail-size row and must be recognisable at 130 px (silhouette test). Props that carry the story are real objects with readable text (signs, labels, banners), not floating boxes.
3. **Places** — each shot is in a place that belongs to the topic; the place is named by an in-scene sign, banner or map pin, or by the label, never only in the narration.
4. **Motion graphics** — map/route, year stamps that slam on the beat, counters, pops. One idea per shot.
5. **FRAME GATE** — `python3 framegate.py <clip>.py` renders 3-5 key frames per shot and checks: F1 no label/stamp/dialogue/caption over a face; F2 label and stamp do not overlap; F3 no person (torso+head) cut by the frame edge; F4 overlays inside the frame; F5 the hook changes visibly; F6 every shot has a person unless listed in `PLACE_SHOTS`. It must print `FRAME GATE: PASS (0 fail, 0 warn)`. Then **look at every review sheet** (`out/<id>/gate/review_*.png`): hero big and readable, nothing important under the caption band (y 1250-1500) or the safe-zone crop (top/bottom 150 px), no stray objects, no giant blobs, jokes read in two seconds. Fix and re-run until both pass.
6. **Cover** — one hero (huge face or object), a recognisable subject, background blurred (mask pass), text <= 5 words inside y 150-1770 and nothing in the bottom 300 px; checked by eye at full size and at 130 px.
7. **Render** — `python3 build_clip.py <clip>.py --render --mix --workers 2` (more workers can time out).
8. **VIDEO GATE** — `python3 videogate.py <id>`: duration 22-45 s (best 25-41), loudness -16.5..-12.5 LUFS, peak <= -1 dBFS, no black frames, first frame = cover. Then watch the first 3 s and one frame per shot of the real file.
9. **Deliver** — send video + cover + the fact table status; list what is still uncertain; post only when the owner says so; then give the owner their to-do list (tags, cover, pinned comment, replies) and measure stayed-to-watch against earlier clips.
10. **Lessons** — an element that fails in review (giant ear on the cover, bandage over the eyes, text over a face, person cut by the edge, caption over the key prop) becomes a rule here or a check in `framegate.py`.

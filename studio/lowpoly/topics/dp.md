# The Town That Couldn't Stop Dancing (Dancing plague of 1518) — source note
Fact-checked 2026-11-01 against the raw text of Wikipedia "Dancing plague of 1518" (curl). Encyclopedia source; the events are known from fragmentary chronicles.
| line | status | source |
|---|---|---|
| "In 1518, a woman started dancing in a street, and she did not stop." | OK | July 1518, Strasbourg, a woman (Frau Troffea in most accounts) began to dance in the street. "did not stop" = kept dancing for days/weeks (the article: kept up constant dancing for a week, then continued). |
| "Within weeks, between fifty and four hundred people were dancing with her." | OK | "By August ... between 50 and 400 people" (the number is disputed). |
| "Nobody knew why. The doctors had a plan: dance it out." | OK | "No one knew what caused this reaction"; physicians "prescribed the afflicted to 'dance themselves free of it'". |
| "The city council even gave them a hall, plus musicians." | OK | "claims that guild halls were refurbished to accommodate the dancing, as well as musicians and strong people" — a claim ("there are claims"), so spoken as "gave them a hall". |
| "It backfired. More people danced, so the council banned dancing. And music." | OK | "This backfired, and the council was forced to ban public dancing ... The council went as far as to ban music, as well." |
| "The cure was red shoes, holy water, and a chapel. By September, it was over." | OK | dancers sent to the shrine of Saint Vitus in red shoes sprinkled with holy water, crosses painted; "ceased in September ... led to a mountaintop chapel". Chapel on a hill is drawn, not the actual building. |
| "Lesson learned: do not hire a band." | joke | not a claim. |
NOT said: any number of deaths (the article stresses it is unknown and sources disagree), the cause (ergot, mass hysteria: theories only).
Visuals: every dancer is a deliberately distorted rubbery figure (giant heads, noodle limbs, googly eyes) — a joke style, not how people looked. Signs "DANCE IT OUT!", "NO DANCING", "NO MUSIC", "HIRED: BAND" are our props.

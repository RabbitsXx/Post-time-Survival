# The Pig War — source note
Script claims -> sources
- "In 1859, an American farmer shot a pig" — Lyman Cutlar, 15 June 1859, San Juan Island. NPS: https://www.nps.gov/sajh/learn/historyculture/the-pig-war.htm ; HistoryLink 5037.
- "it belonged to a British company" — the pig belonged to the Hudson's Bay Company (NPS).
- "It was eating his garden" — pig rooting in Cutlar's garden (NPS).
- "Britain threatened to arrest him" — British authorities threatened to arrest Cutlar (NPS).
- "America sent soldiers / Britain sent warships" — Brig. Gen. William Harney sent Capt. George Pickett's company; Britain sent warships (NPS, HistoryLink).
- "hundreds of soldiers and ships" — kept vague on purpose (US troops grew to several hundred; several British warships).
- "nobody else was hurt / only casualty was the pig" — NPS: no human casualties.
- "twelve years ... German emperor" — joint military occupation ~12 years; arbitration by Kaiser Wilhelm I of Germany in 1872 awarded the islands to the US (NPS).
- Final line is a joke (no claim).

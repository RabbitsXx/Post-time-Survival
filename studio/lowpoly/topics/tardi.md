# tardi — The Tiny Animal That Survived Outer Space (source: Wikipedia "Tardigrade", raw text fetched 2026-11-02)
| # | spoken line | status | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A tiny animal once survived ten days in outer space, with no spacesuit. | OK | "They have survived exposure to outer space"; FOTON-M3 2007: exposed to vacuum/UV for 10 days |
| 2 | It is called a tardigrade, or water bear, a microscopic creature with eight legs. | OK | "also known as water bears or moss piglets, are a phylum of eight-legged segmented micro-animals" |
| 3 | In 2007, scientists launched thirty of them, dried out, into orbit. | OK | "In 2007, samples of 30 tardigrades and 30 eggs, all dehydrated, were taken on the FOTON-M3 mission" |
| 4 | Empty space alone did not hurt them. Add the Sun's ultraviolet light, and many died. | OK | "Vacuum exposure alone had no significant effect on any metric, but, when combined with UV exposure, killed many of the tuns" |
| 5 | But some came back to life when they got wet again. | OK | "only 1 R. coronifer specimen and 68% of the M. tardigradum specimens exposed to the limited UV range were reanimated by rehydration" (mortality high in following days) |
| 6 | They can even dry out completely and wait for years, until water returns. | OK | cryptobiotic "tun" state: "can go without food or water for several years" |
| 7 | Not bad for something nicknamed a moss piglet. | OK | "water bears or moss piglets" |
Note: survival under FULL solar UV was much lower (only a few animals); the clip says "many died" and "some came back".

# Wombats Poop Cubes — source note
- Yang et al., "Intestines of non-uniform stiffness mold the corners of wombat feces", Soft Matter 17, 475-488 (2021) (Georgia Tech + Univ. of Tasmania). Coverage: Smithsonian Magazine, Live Science, CNN (29 Jan 2021), UTAS news 28 Jan 2021.
- Facts used: cube-shaped droppings; intestine has stiff and flexible (stretchy) regions; they mold the dung into cubes as it dries in the last part of the intestine.
- NOT stated as fact: territory marking on rocks/logs is a hypothesis ("scientists suspect"). Quantity "dozens a night" is reported (about 80-100 cubes) — kept vague.
- CORRECTION to earlier idea list: 2018 conference abstract said cubes form in last ~8% and 4 stiff + 4 flexible zones; the 2021 paper revised this (two stiff, two flexible; shaping in the last ~17%). Script avoids these numbers.

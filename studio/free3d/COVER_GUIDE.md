# SHORTS COVER GUIDE (researched; sources are third-party blogs, not YouTube docs — check the Shorts help page before relying on exact numbers)
Sources: piktochart.com/blog/youtube-shorts-dimensions · growthos.in/blog/youtube-shorts-thumbnails-size · instasize.com/size-guides/youtube/shorts-thumbnail · timeskip.io/blog/youtube-shorts-thumbnail-size · creatorr.pages.dev (Shorts thumbnail strategy) · entreresource.com/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices · thumbfa.st (psychology of clickable thumbnails) · tubecore.xpanddigital.io (thumbnails & CTR)

## Facts (agreed by most sources)
- 1080×1920 (9:16), JPG/PNG, <2 MB. A 16:9 upload is centre-cropped to ~405/720 width, so keep subjects central.
- Some feeds/grids crop to a ~1:1 centre square → keep face + hook words inside y 420–1500 (1080 px square).
- Keep the bottom ~300 px empty (channel name, caption, buttons) and don't rely on edges.
- ≤5 words, bold sans, thick outline, high contrast; test at phone-thumbnail size.
- Expressive faces + a curiosity gap (show a question, not the answer); one focal point; avoid clutter.
- Judge by CTR (A/B), not by taste. Percent lifts quoted online (20–40%) are vendor claims.

## Our template (Last Bus layout, adapted)
top-left series capsule · top-right PART N (yellow) · 2-line headline (white / yellow) inside the centre square · red pill just under it · bottom 300 px left dark and empty.
Variant A = calm smiling face with the dead right behind her shoulder (curiosity). Variant B = fear vs calm (Gus screaming, Mimi smiling). Use A first, B as the A/B test.

# REMAIN CALM · Part 1 — THE DEAD SMELL FEAR

**Logline:** A cashier whose smile never breaks must teach a jumpy security guard to stay calm before the dead — who can smell fear — find him.

**What it is really about:** calm is contagious; fear spreads faster than the dead.

**Emotional arc:** deadpan normality → creeping dread → panic (Gus) → learning calm → complication (Rosa) → cliff.

**Director's rules:** no narrator · ≤3.5 s average shot · every shot has a purpose (below) · silence before reveals (3 gates) · rule of three (Carrot) · plant & payoff (PA line) · loop ending · hero frames: front for emotion, profile for conversation, back for dread.
